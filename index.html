ℹ️ System Quick Operational Guide

Tab 1: Log Sourcing Transaction

  • Master CSV: Load deduplicated survey CSV file (Yield auto-set to 3kg/tree).
  • Header Entry: Enter Vendor, GRN (auto-UPPERCASE), Total Qty, Date, Location, Staff Name.
  • Proximity Search: Input Vendor Lat/Lng, set radius (1-100 km), click Find Villages.
  • Allocation & Undo: Allocate per village or click ⚡ Add All Entered. Multiple additions to same village merge automatically into 1 row. Click & type in staged box to edit live.
  • 0 Capacity Display: Fully sourced villages ($0\text{ KG}$ remaining) remain visible in table.
  • Reset: Click 🧹 Reset Form to prepare for next truck while keeping CSV attached.

Tab 2: Audit & PDF Reports

  • Independent Filters: Filter strictly by GRN Number OR Vendor Name.
  • Delete GRN: Click 🗑️ Delete Selected GRN to permanently delete a specific transaction without wiping device cache.
  • High-Legibility PDF: Font size increased to 13px–14px for print readability. 14 villages max per page prevents clipping.
  • Consolidated PDF Reports: Download Date Range or Vendor-Specific combined multi-sheet PDF statements at the bottom of Tab 2.
