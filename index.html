ℹ️ How This System Works (Field Sourcing & Traceability Workflow) Click to Expand / Collapse

Step 1 (Master Setup): Load your deduplicated survey CSV file. System calculates village yield limits ( Trees × 3 kg ). Change unit between KG and MT at top right as needed.

Step 2 (Transaction Header): Enter Vendor Name, Truck Arrival ID / GRN (auto-capitalized), Total Procured Weight, Date, Location, and Supervisor Name.

Step 3 (Proximity Lookup): Enter Vendor / Town Latitude & Longitude, adjust radius slider (1 to 100 km), and click Find Villages in Radius.

Step 4 (Flexible Allocation & Undo): Allocate weights to nearby villages individually or via ⚡ Add All Entered. If an error is made, click 🗑️ Remove in the staged items table to undo and restore capacity.

Step 5 (10% Soft Cap & Live Balance): A +10% negative deviation soft cap allows minor operational variance without blocking entries. If total assigned weight exceeds procurement, the balance indicator turns RED.

Step 6 (Save & Cloud Sync): Click Save Transaction to instantly append data to your central Google Sheet across all supervisors' devices.

ℹ️ How Tab 2 (Single & Date-to-Date Consolidated PDF Reports) Works Click to Expand / Collapse

1. Single GRN Search & Download: Select a specific Truck Arrival ID / GRN or Vendor Name from the dropdown to view and download an individual A4 certificate.

2. Consolidated Date-to-Date Download (Bank Statement Style): Set a From Date and To Date. Clicking 📄 Download Consolidated PDF Report will generate a merged master PDF containing all GRNs in chronological order by Date and GRN Number.

3. Multi-Page Handling: Every individual GRN formatted within the merged report respects max 15 lines per page and cleanly breaks across pages.

4. Dynamic Units & CSV Export: All PDF exports and global CSV downloads automatically format in your selected unit (KG / MT).
