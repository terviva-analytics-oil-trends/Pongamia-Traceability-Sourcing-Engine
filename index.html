ℹ️ How This System Works (Field Sourcing & Traceability Workflow) Click to Expand / Collapse

Step 1 (Master Setup): Load your deduplicated survey CSV file. System calculates village yield limits ( Trees × 3 kg ). Change unit between KG and MT at top right as needed.

Step 2 (Transaction Header): Enter Vendor Name, Truck Arrival ID / GRN, Total Procured Weight, Date, Location, and Supervisor Name.

Step 3 (Proximity Lookup): Enter Vendor / Town Latitude & Longitude, adjust radius slider (1 to 100 km), and click Find Villages in Radius.

Step 4 (Flexible Allocation): Allocate weights to nearby villages using either Add (individual village) or type quantities across multiple villages and click ⚡ Add All Entered at once.

Step 5 (10% Soft Cap & Live Balance): A +10% negative deviation soft cap allows minor operational variance without blocking entries. If total assigned weight exceeds procurement, the balance indicator turns RED.

Step 6 (Save & Cloud Sync): Click Save Transaction at the top or bottom of the table to instantly append data to your central Google Sheet across all supervisors' devices.

ℹ️ How Tab 2 (Traceability Audit & PDF Generation) Works Click to Expand / Collapse

1. Search & Filter: Select a Truck Arrival ID / GRN or Vendor Name from the dropdown menu to immediately view all associated village allocations.

2. Printable A4 Layout (15 Lines Max per Page): The generated certificate strictly formats data into standard A4 document layout. If a transaction contains more than 15 villages, it automatically breaks across multiple pages cleanly.

3. Vendor Signature & Supervisor Logging: Form includes a clean Vendor Signature line alongside supervisor logging details at the bottom of the page.

4. Export Options: Click Download A4 PDF Certificate for official audit compliance or Export All Logged Data (CSV) to download the entire consolidated dataset in selected units (KG / MT).
